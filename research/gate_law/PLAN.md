Exact prompts, templates, follow-ups, budgets and token IDs are frozen in the input manifest before running a candidate. Prefill ends immediately before the last known prompt token. The anchor is the actual layer16 gate at the last consumed source token. The candidate consumes the pending known prompt token to choose its first answer. No native answer token, future cache or refreshed anchor enters the law.

For each channel store FP32 source g*, p*=1/(1+exp(-g*)), c1=p*(1-p*), c2=(0.5*c1)*(1-2*p*) and s*=g*/(1+exp(-g*)). The law computes d=g-g*, sigma=(p*+c1*d)+(c2*d)*d and s=s*+(g*sigma-g**p*) with FP32 operations and contraction disabled, followed by the actual up product and dense down map. Its arithmetic grouping, coefficient bytes and source clock are part of the program. There is no global accuracy assumption.

No sampling, training, reanchoring or automatic fallback occurs.
