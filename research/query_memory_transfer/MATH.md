# A value approximation crosses a memory approximation

At selected layer17 let the actually normalized lower-layer receiver be x_t. The frozen source basis B has128 rows. A factored map is `W_hat x_t = (W B^T)(B x_t)` with the stored FP32 image/profile. The native RoPE, attention output projection, MLP and later suffix remain active. The compact policy's x_t is generated by its own consumed IDs through the native lower stack; it is not supplied from a saved native target trace.

For one query head, full attention is

\[
Y=P(q,K)V,\qquad P_i=\frac{\exp(q^Tk_i/\sqrt{128})}{\sum_j\exp(q^Tk_j/\sqrt{128})}.
\]

The bounded reader retains16 recent exact rows and16 counted prototypes per KV group. Prototype C has count m_C, key mean kbar_C and value mean vbar_C. Its node has score `q·kbar_C/sqrt(128)+log(m_C)`. Expanding a node's normalized weight w_C across its historical members defines lifted weights `Ptilde_i=w_C/m_C`. These memberships exist only in the diagnostic replay; the C serving state keeps sums/counts and the recent ring.

Compiled Q/K are common to CC and CN. Their keys and key-only clustering are identical while their consumed histories and normalized receivers match. Thus, in ideal arithmetic,

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
Y_{CN,F}=PV_N,&Y_{CC,F}=PV_C,\\
Y_{CN,C}=\widetilde P V_N,&Y_{CC,C}=\widetilde P V_C.
\end{array}
\]

The memory-by-value interaction factors as

\[
I=Y_{CC,C}-Y_{CC,F}-Y_{CN,C}+Y_{CN,F}
=(\widetilde P-P)(V_C-V_N).
\]

This identifies a product of two approximation errors conditional on shared Q/K and partition. It is not a statement that the errors add independently, that the interaction harms fidelity, or that full-model logits are a linear image of I.

Let each actual saved output be `Y_ab = W_ab V_ab + R_ab`, using actual native full FP32 probabilities and independently reconstructed FP64 compact weights. Then

\[
I_{actual}=(\widetilde P-P)(V_C-V_N)
+R_{CC,C}-R_{CC,F}-R_{CN,C}+R_{CN,F}+\epsilon_{audit}.
\]

The measured remainders include mean/value accumulation, division and reduction order and FP32 casting. The tiny audit expansion remainder is retained separately. Before row33 no prototype has multiple members, so the compact/full discrepancy there is a numerical-profile contrast. After row33 it also includes aggregation information loss.

To ask whether native-V benefit survives compression, choose one fixed native full-model logit reference z_N and define

\[
G_F=\frac{\|z_{CC,F}-z_N\|^2-\|z_{CN,F}-z_N\|^2}{\|z_N\|^2},
\quad
G_C=\frac{\|z_{CC,C}-z_N\|^2-\|z_{CN,C}-z_N\|^2}{\|z_N\|^2}.
\]

Positive gain is improvement in this observable. `G_C-G_F` retains its sign. All four readout clocks are measured even after different tokens are consumed, but those later comparisons describe complete recurring policies with different inputs and memory. Common IDs alone still do not imply identical upper-layer caches.

For a prototype, write `k_i=kbar+delta_k_i`, `v_i=vbar+delta_v_i`, and `a_i=q·delta_k_i/sqrt(128)`. The exact cluster response before its global softmax denominator is

\[
N_C=e^{q^T\bar k/\sqrt{128}}
\left[\bar v\sum_{i\in C}e^{a_i}+\sum_{i\in C}e^{a_i}\delta v_i\right],
\quad
D_C=e^{q^T\bar k/\sqrt{128}}\sum_{i\in C}e^{a_i}.
\]

The centroid reader substitutes `sum exp(a_i)≈m` and `sum exp(a_i)delta_v_i≈0`. Restoring native V does not restore these omitted terms. Whether any covariance moment or query-sensitive split is worthwhile must be judged through a later reader, its carried-state trajectory and its actual cost. This experiment measures transfer before adding either mechanism.

The remaining addressing error can be expanded without another model call. On the initial common history, with the actual post-RoPE operands, let `delta_q=q_C−q_N` and `delta_K=K_C−K_N`. Then

\[
\Delta s=\frac{\delta q K_N^T+q_N\delta K^T+\delta q\delta K^T}{\sqrt{128}}.
\]

Softmax ignores a constant added to all positions of one query/head. Therefore retain both raw scores and row-centered scores when comparing these signed terms. Their norms do not independently assign causal responsibility; their cross terms and subsequent probability/value response matter. A saved FP64 score expansion is an analysis of recorded operands, not byte-exact replay of the native CUDA attention or an executed Q-only/K-only repair. Any proposed repair still needs its own recurring-policy test.
