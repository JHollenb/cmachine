## How this changes the hypothesis

The positive result is a full-layer, from-empty, own-state encoded-memory loop with meaningful allocation savings and relatively modest byte-full readout drift on the matched histories. The selective-reading lead also survives in a limited form: top-four retains recognizable answers and conditional reads much better than the recent window. It remains markedly less faithful than byte-full and does not implement the required exchange.

The result does not establish a fixed-size sufficient memory, a finite feature factorization of the exponential kernel, cheap original operand formation, broad semantic state closure, or forward-pass-free execution. All native learned maps still execute. Top-four retains full memory; recent-four is fixed-size but loses the admitted computation.

The constructive feature-state law in the hypothesis discussion remains a mathematical proposal: if an economical admitted factorization K(q,k)=phi(q)^T psi(k) exists, then z=sum psi(k) and B=sum psi(k)v^T close under append and answer admitted queries. This panel neither constructs that factorization nor establishes a uniform relative kernel-error bound. Its evidence favors preserving query-qualified paired associations, not a particular finite feature algebra.
